LIQUID PHASE

Single-phase
immersion

Servers remain submerged in dielectric fluid that stays liquid during normal operation. Heat transfers through fluid circulation and a heat exchanger or external cooling loop.

  • Fluid circulation and heat exchange
  • Fluid compatibility and maintenance
  • Tank, piping and instrumentation design

PHASE CHANGE

Two-phase
immersion

Dielectric fluid boils at the operating heat load and vapour condenses back into liquid. Design depends on fluid properties, containment and heat rejection.

  • Boiling and condensation cycle
  • Containment and fluid management
  • Application-specific component selection
