02TECHNOLOGY

Two approaches to
moving heat.

Single-phase and two-phase immersion cooling use different thermal mechanisms. Actual suitability depends on the equipment, fluid, heat load, operating environment and engineering requirements.

ARCHITECTURE / A≈

LIQUID CIRCULATION

Single-phase immersion

The dielectric fluid remains liquid during normal operation. Heat is carried away by fluid circulation and transferred through a heat exchanger or external cooling loop.

03BEFORE YOU SELECT A SYSTEM

Start with the
right constraints.

Immersion cooling is a system decision, not a standalone tank purchase. The architecture and supporting equipment depend on your IT hardware, facility conditions, chosen fluid and operating requirements.

01

Workload and IT hardware

Server form factor, workload, power density, service access and manufacturer support requirements.

02

Cooling architecture

Single-phase or two-phase behaviour, thermal path, heat-rejection interface and system boundaries.

03

Fluid and materials compatibility

Exact fluid specifications, wetted materials, seals, cables, connectors, warranties and handling requirements.

04

Operations and evidence

Instrumentation, maintenance approach, safety documentation, supplier support and acceptance criteria.

05MONITORING / FUTURE EXPLORATION

Operational visibility
starts with good evidence.

A sensor-connected software layer is a possible future direction, not a current STURN product. Hardware, software, integrations, security design and service arrangements have not been selected or validated.

01

Choose meaningful measurements

Candidate signals may include temperature, fluid level, flow, pressure or leak detection. Relevance and sensor suitability depend on the selected system.

02

Validate the data path

Device accuracy, calibration, connectivity, data quality, units and integration requirements need to be defined before any readings are trusted.

03

Design for security and ownership

Access control, encryption, retention, export/deletion, incident response and customer data ownership must be agreed before a pilot.

No connected installations, live telemetry, predictive maintenance, energy savings, remote control or production SaaS capability is claimed.

10COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear about where
things stand.

Is STURN currently trading?

STURN is developing its supplier network and commercial offer. Product availability, supplier relationships and commercial terms have not been confirmed on this website.

Does STURN currently offer immersion-cooling products?

No products or stock are represented as currently available from STURN. The site describes technology categories and the business direction under development.

Is the monitoring dashboard connected to equipment?

No. The dashboard is an illustrative interface using demonstration values. A production monitoring service would require selected hardware, data integrations, a software partner and validation.

Can manufacturers discuss distribution opportunities?

Yes. STURN welcomes early conversations with immersion-cooling manufacturers, component specialists, instrumentation providers, fluid suppliers and monitoring technology providers.

Does this website provide engineering advice?

No. It provides general educational explanations. Equipment selection, fluid compatibility, thermal sizing, safety and installation decisions require qualified engineering and manufacturer guidance.
